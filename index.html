Resilient civic connectivity

When a network fails, communities stay connected.

CIVINET helps essential messages reach people during network disruptions by connecting telecom networks, public facilities and local communities.

The problem

During an outage, sending an alert is not enough.

Responders need to know what still works, where people are isolated and whether important information reached them.

01Communication breaks

Network failures can interrupt access to updates and essential services.

02Communities become invisible

A message marked “sent” does not mean every neighbourhood received it.

03Local resources go unused

Nearby facilities may still have power and connectivity but are not coordinated.

The CIVINET response

Four steps from disruption to confirmation.

CIVINET coordinates available connections and shows responders where communication is still missing.

1

Detect

Identify an outage and the areas at risk of losing contact.

2

Find resources

Locate participating facilities with power and connectivity.

3

Reroute

Send priority information through an available alternative path.

4

Verify

Check delivery acknowledgements and flag areas still unreached.
