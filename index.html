The challenge

Connectivity failure is also a community failure.

When communication infrastructure is disrupted, people lose access to instructions, services lose coordination and entire neighbourhoods can become difficult to reach.

01 / FRAGMENTED NETWORKS

One failure creates many gaps

Mobile, Wi-Fi, public facilities and local response teams often operate separately, even when they could support one another.

02 / INVISIBLE ISOLATION

Unreached areas go unnoticed

A broadcast can be marked as sent while some communities remain disconnected or unable to receive it.

03 / NO SHARED RESPONSE

Local resources stay disconnected

Facilities with power or internet may be nearby, but there is no coordinated way to turn them into temporary communication points.

The platform

One civic layer. Multiple networks. A shared response.

CIVINET coordinates the resources that still work and uses them to reconnect the people and services that need them.

Core capabilities

More than an alert system.

CIVINET coordinates connectivity and community response, rather than simply publishing information.

⌁

Connectivity intelligence

Build a live picture of network availability and identify where normal communication is deteriorating.

▦

Dynamic community gateways

Enable participating libraries, schools, pharmacies and community centres to act as temporary information relay points.

⇄

Adaptive message routing

Route critical information through available channels, with priority based on urgency and delivery requirements.

✚

Community resource exchange

Coordinate verified offers of shelter, charging, transport assistance and volunteer support.

◉

Delivery and reachability

Separate sent, delivered and acknowledged states so responders can see where communication is still missing.

⌑

Privacy-aware participation

Use opt-in gateways, minimum necessary data and aggregated network signals instead of tracking individual residents.

Network-native foundation

Where programmable networks meet public resilience.

Nokia's networking expertise provides a relevant foundation for the connectivity layer. Network APIs and orchestration capabilities could help participating operators expose network status and request suitable connectivity profiles, where supported.
